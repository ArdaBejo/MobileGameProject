# Week 8 Lab B resilience test plan

8 November 2026. Pixel 8 Android Emulator, Android 16 / API 36, x86-64 guest. Signed ARM64 IL2CPP release v0.8.0 (version code 5), build ID 49ea902f. Results apply to this emulator run. The later Music settings source changes were not part of this APK.

| # | Scenario | Trigger | Expected | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Resume / background | Home for 30 s, return, repeat ten times, then force-stop and relaunch | Pauses with audio silent, resumes in place and preserves save | Pass on emulator for visual resume and save | All ten 30-second returns kept PID 5697 and resumed after pressing Resume. [Cycle 10](screens/resume-10-return.png) and [new arena after force-stop](screens/relaunch-arena.png) show 2423 coins and the same upgrade price. Background audio was not independently measured. |
| 2 | No network | Cold launch with airplane mode on; turn airplane mode off and back on during the arena | Menu and arena remain responsive and no unhandled exception stops play | Pass on emulator | [Offline arena](screens/offline-game.png), [mid-play](screens/offline-midplay.png), and offline markers in Logcat |
| 3 | Rotation | Disable auto-rotate and request landscape during play | Portrait lock holds and arena HUD retains its layout | Pass on emulator | [Rotation screenshot](screens/rotation.png), arena countdown and HUD visible |
| 4 | Battery Saver | Enable Battery Saver and play for two minutes | Gameplay remains responsive, FPS cap and overlay reflect saver mode | Fail on emulator | [Android reported saver ON](battery-state.txt), but after about 105 seconds of active play the [overlay still targeted 120 FPS](screens/battery-end.png). The system mode was on for over two minutes including the title screen. The original setting was restored. |
| 5 | Low RAM | Request memory trim, background with other apps running, and return | No black screen, or saved progress restored after a kill | Pass on emulator for the exercised paths | A short trim returned to the [paused arena](screens/trim-return.png). A second trim followed by Camera and Chrome for 30 seconds each returned to the [title screen](screens/full-trim-return.png) with PID 6736 retained. No low-memory kill was observed. |
| 6 | Cold and warm start | Force-stop and launch with timing, then Home for ten seconds and relaunch | Record launch times and verify the loading screen clears | Pass for the specified cold and Home-relaunch procedure | Cold 1048 ms. Home relaunch 193 ms with the same PID, classified HOT by Android. Menu interactive at 13.30 s Unity uptime. Arena loaded in 1564 ms. A follow-up cold launch took 8993 ms and reached the menu at 18.79 s Unity uptime. A forced task recreation returned UNKNOWN, with no valid WARM TotalTime. |
