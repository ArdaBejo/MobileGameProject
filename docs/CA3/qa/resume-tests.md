## Scope of the three checks

State preservation and continued gameplay are captured above. Development samples verify the global audio pause flag and scaled clock, not physical speaker output. Source audit and resumed spawn samples support one wave-loop restart; they do not prove every possible timer interleaving. [Awaitable audit](awaitable-audit.md) explains cancellation and the intentional navigation/screenshot exceptions.

The existing sideload startup `ClassNotFoundException` for Play AssetPackManager appears again on cold launch. It is already tracked in KnownIssues.md and is not a newly introduced resume exception. No new managed exception/ANR was observed in these exercised paths. This does not prove absence of all crashes.

The incoming-call row is deliberately open. Neither simulated telephony nor the AP emulator overlay is substituted for physical-device evidence.
