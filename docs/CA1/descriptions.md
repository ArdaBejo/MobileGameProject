# No Stars Remain — Store Descriptions

**Version:** 0.3.0  
**Module:** Mobile Game Development  
**Status:** CA1 Publication Planning

## 1. Short Description (Max. 80 Characters)

Survive alien waves in a dark sci-fi arena-survivor roguelite.

## 2. Long Description

**No Stars Remain** is a dark sci-fi arena-survivor roguelite set in a hostile alien universe.

Take control of a lone survivor stranded on a desolate planet. Face increasingly dangerous waves of alien enemies, improve your abilities and fight to survive.

**Planned Gameplay Features:**

- **Automatic Combat:** Focus on movement and positioning while your weapons attack automatically.
- **Three Unique Weapons:** Fight using the Pistol, Laser Sword and Rocket Launcher.
- **Character Upgrades:** Improve your damage, movement speed and survivability through passive upgrades.
- **Challenging Enemies:** Encounter increasingly dangerous alien creatures and powerful bosses.
- **Roguelite Progression:** Experiment with different upgrades and strategies across repeated survival attempts.
- **Atmospheric Sci-Fi Setting:** Explore a dark, isolated universe with hostile planetary environments.

Every encounter challenges your positioning, survival skills and upgrade choices.

How long can you survive when no stars remain?

## 3. Publication Status

No Stars Remain is currently in development. The descriptions above represent the intended game experience, including features planned for the semester project.

The current CA1 prototype contains a splash screen, main menu and initial gameplay scene. The remaining features will be implemented and tested during development.

No Google Play Store upload is planned for CA1.
