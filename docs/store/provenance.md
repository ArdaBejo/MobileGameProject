# Store asset provenance

The icon and feature graphic are promotional artwork generated with OpenAI ImageGen on 25 November 2026, using the project's Android arena screenshot as a character and palette reference. Exports were resized to the required dimensions and their channel formats were checked. The icon's subject is one white astronaut helmet with a black visor and cyan reflection.

The gameplay screenshots are captured from the Android Development player with StoreShot. Its central 9:16 crop excludes the tall-screen edges and Development Build watermark. It temporarily hides every enabled Canvas and the player's world-space health bar, then restores them after encoding. The arena, enemies, player positions and bullets come from gameplay.

Icon readability was inspected by the assistant at 48×48 pixels. A two-second recognition response from another person has not been collected.

The project sources remain under their existing ownership. Generated promotional artwork should be considered separately from runtime screenshot evidence.
